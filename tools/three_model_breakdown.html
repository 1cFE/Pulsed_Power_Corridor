Five D-T fusion concepts, all costed by 1costingFE with identical plant assumptions (1 GWe, 0.85 availability, single chamber for all — including MagLIF, for methodological parity), differing only in driver, drive mode, and wall. Fast-ignition concepts (Focused Energy) carry a gain credit (~1.5× central-hot-spot); direct-drive concepts run an honest HAPL dry wall (f_wall=1). Same palette as the model tab; open circle marks each concept's LCOE-optimal rep rate.  ·  Xcimer (cheap KrF driver, $40/J) bottoms out lowest and at low rep (~2 Hz) — its economic case rides on that aggressive driver cost. BLF is shown with both a dry and a liquid (wet) wall, now with a wall-dependent first-wall life (dry 2.5 FPY, advanced 4, thick-liquid ~plant-life) rather than the stock flat 5 FPY. The wet wall shrinks the chamber, pulls the optimum from ~12 Hz back to ~5 Hz, and nearly zeroes first-wall replacement. Note the gap widens mostly by penalizing the dry wall, not crediting the liquid one (whose FW replacement was already small — so liquid is not over-credited). And the liquid wall's own hidden costs are still NOT charged here: recirculating pump power (parasitic load), FLiBe/PbLi inventory + primary-loop capital, tritium extraction, and chamber vapor/droplet clearing between shots — real costs, not just risk, so the wet wall's true net edge is smaller than these panels show. Inertia's indirect (hohlraum) drive inflates both the driver (poorer coupling → bigger driver) and the target line. Focused Energy keeps the same DPSSL laser but switches to direct drive (fast ignition): better coupling shrinks the driver versus the hohlraum case, so it lands between BLF and Inertia (wall assumed advanced-solid). MagLIF is now sized like the lasers (single chamber): a near-free cap-bank driver with a consumable (recyclable transmission line + liner) that rises with rep, so its optimum sits at low rep — competitive on cost. Zap (sheared-flow Z-pinch) is the other pulsed-power concept — a near-free cap-bank driver and cheap chamber, but at a fixed gain its per-shot electrode consumable climbs with rep, so like MagLIF it optimizes at low rep (~0.5 Hz), cost-competitive — with the same fixed-gain / pulsed-power feasibility risk not priced here.  ·  Driver = laser C220104 or pulsed-power cap-bank C220107; chamber = FW/blanket + shield + vessel. Cost only. The real MagLIF risk is not priced here and lives on the driver: every shot delivers a ~100 MJ current pulse through a consumed transmission line to a single liner — the part that does not scale or rep-rate like external laser beams (feasibility, not $).
