* How q_eng is derived. q_eng is derived from each concept's driver efficiency, target gain, and conversion efficiency. The target gain is approximated assuming that each concept's burn follows standard ICF physics for its drive mode and size. Gain is derived indirectly from the driver energy per shot using the equations below:
gain = φ × (fuel loaded per driver joule) × coupling × eDT
where:
φ = ρR / (ρR + HB)  and  ρR ∝ E1/3
fuel loaded per driver joule = 1.1 mg/MJ
coupling = 1.0 for direct drive, <0.5 for indirect configs
eDT = specific fusion energy, ~3.4×105 MJ/g for D-T fuel
† How the annualized consumable cost is computed. It is the levelized annual fuel-cycle cost — the fabricated target consumed each shot plus the raw isotope fuel — so, unlike a per-shot figure, it is rep-rate-normalized (a low-rep concept firing few large targets can beat a high-rep one firing many cheap ones):
annual cost = ( target $/shot × shots/yr  +  isotope fuel $/yr ) × L
where:
shots/yr = f_rep × availability × 3.15×107 s × n_mod,  availability = 0.85
target $/shot scales with assembled fuel mass (capsule / liner + RTL)
isotope fuel: D-T breeds its tritium (buys D + Li-6, ≈ free)
L ≈ 1.36 = levelization (interest + inflation over plant life);
   unburned, unrecovered isotope fuel is repurchased
